South Florida Airport & Private Jet Transfers

From your flight to your next stop, travel with a professional chauffeur. Splendid Limo provides airport and private aviation transportation across Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and West Palm Beach.

Plan around your airport and destination

The airport, passenger count and luggage all shape your trip. Tell us where you are arriving and the full address of your destination so we can discuss the route and suitable vehicle.

  • Miami International Airport (MIA): transfers for stays and meetings in Miami, including South Beach and Brickell, or onward travel in South Florida.
  • Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL): arrivals and departures for Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and the surrounding service area.
  • Palm Beach International Airport (PBI): transportation for West Palm Beach, Boca Raton and other destinations within our service area.

Flight tracking and pickup coordination

Our chauffeurs use flight tracking to help coordinate airport pickups. Include the airline, flight number, travel date and a mobile number where we can reach you. For international arrivals, mention that you will need time for immigration and baggage collection.

Confirm your meeting point and pickup instructions with our team before you travel. If your itinerary changes, contact us with the updated flight details. Ask about waiting time, parking and any extra charges when reviewing your quote; do not assume they are included.

Private aviation and FBO transfers

We coordinate with fixed-base operators (FBOs) for private jet pickups and drop-offs. Share the FBO name and address, arrival time, passenger count and luggage needs. Confirm the meeting location with our team; access arrangements vary by facility.

Choose a vehicle for passengers and luggage

Our fleet includes the Cadillac Escalade, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Bentley Bentayga and Mercedes-Benz Maybach. Tell us about checked bags, carry-ons or bulky equipment before choosing a vehicle. We’ll discuss availability and fit for your particular trip rather than relying on a model name alone.

What to send with your request

  1. Airport or FBO, flight number and travel date.
  2. Pickup and drop-off addresses, including any additional stops.
  3. Passenger count, luggage and any special requirements to confirm.
  4. A contact number and whether you also need a return transfer.

For a day of meetings after arrival, explore executive car service. Planning a stay in Boca Raton? Read our Boca Raton transportation guide.

Tell us your date, route and group size. We’ll follow up by phone or text to discuss your trip and quote.
