Wedding & Event Transportation in South Florida

Bring your transportation plans together for a wedding, birthday or special celebration. Our chauffeurs serve Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and West Palm Beach.

Request a QuoteCall 954-669-8444

Build the ride around your schedule

For weddings, share the preparation location, ceremony and reception addresses, arrival deadlines and final drop-off. Include any photo stops or separate return journeys you would like us to consider. A clear timeline helps the team discuss the right arrangement for your day.

For birthdays and other special events, let us know the venue, group size, start and finish times, and any additional stops. If your plans involve a night out, compare our Miami party-bus packages.

Choose the vehicle with your group in mind

Explore the Splendid Limo fleet, including the Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Cadillac Escalade, Mercedes-Benz Maybach, Bentley Bentayga and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter. Tell us who will travel together and what they need to bring. Confirm passenger capacity, availability and any special requirements with our team for your date.

Do not assume that decorations, refreshments or a particular vehicle are included. Ask about the details that matter to your celebration when reviewing your quote.

Help guests arriving from out of town

Guests flying into South Florida can request airport transfers separately from the event-day transportation. Provide flight details and hotel addresses, and explain how those journeys fit into your schedule.

A checklist for your event quote

  1. Event date and all pickup and drop-off addresses.
  2. Passenger count for each journey, including any separate groups.
  3. Required arrival times and planned photo or venue stops.
  4. A day-of contact who can coordinate with the chauffeur.
  5. Return plans, special requirements and questions about inclusions.

Review the plan before confirming

Ask our team to confirm the itinerary, assigned vehicle arrangements, total price, payment terms, waiting time and cancellation or change policy. A quote request starts the conversation; it does not by itself reserve a vehicle.
