Boca Raton Limo Service: A Practical Trip Planning Guide

By Splendid Limo & Transportation · Published 2024-08-14 · Updated October 22, 2026

Splendid Limo serves Boca Raton as part of its South Florida service area, alongside Miami, Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach. Whether you are arriving by air, going to a meeting or planning a celebration, a complete itinerary makes it easier to discuss the right ride.

Flying to or from Boca Raton

Tell us which airport you are using: Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL), Palm Beach International (PBI) or Miami International (MIA). Include the flight number, date and your Boca Raton destination address. For a departure, provide the flight time so you can discuss pickup timing with the team rather than relying on a fixed journey estimate.

Our airport transfer service includes flight tracking. For private aviation, provide the FBO name, address and arrival details so pickup coordination can be discussed. Confirm the meeting point, baggage needs and any waiting-time terms before traveling.

Meetings and business travel

If you have more than one meeting, send all addresses and required arrival times together. Include the hotel or airport transfer and the return journey if needed. Our executive car service covers business transportation across the region, so the itinerary can include stops outside Boca Raton as well.

For regular guest transportation, hotels and businesses can discuss corporate partnership arrangements with our team.

Weddings, birthdays and evenings out

For an event, plan who is traveling together and when each group must arrive. Include ceremony, reception or venue addresses, any photo stops and the final drop-off. Use our wedding and event transportation checklist to prepare a request.

If your celebration continues in Miami, review our party-bus packages. Confirm the pickup arrangement, group size and inclusions for your itinerary; do not assume the package automatically covers every route or request.

Choose the vehicle after counting people and bags

Our fleet includes luxury SUVs, the Mercedes-Benz Maybach and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter. Share luggage, equipment and any special requirements along with your passenger count. Ask the team which vehicles are available for your date and confirm the capacity of the particular option.

Questions to settle before booking

  • Which addresses and stops are included in the quote?
  • What are the pickup and required arrival times?
  • How are waiting time, extra stops and changes handled?
  • What payment and cancellation terms apply?
  • Who should the chauffeur contact on the day?

Request a quote with your travel details, then review the itinerary and terms with the team. A request is the start of planning, not a confirmed vehicle reservation.

Plan your ride

Tell us your date, route and group size. We’ll follow up by phone or text to discuss your trip and quote.
