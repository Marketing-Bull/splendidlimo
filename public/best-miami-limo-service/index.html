Choosing a Miami Limo Service: Vehicles, Routes & Quotes

By Splendid Limo & Transportation · Published 2024-08-14 · Updated October 22, 2026

A flight arrival, a business meeting in Brickell and a night out in South Beach need different transportation plans. Start with the route and the people traveling, then choose the vehicle. This guide explains what to discuss with Splendid Limo before confirming your ride.

Start with the itinerary, not just the car

Send the pickup and destination addresses, your date and the time you need to arrive. Include additional stops, whether you need a return trip and anything that may affect loading or access. A journey to one destination and a schedule of meetings should be discussed as different requests.

For business travel, our executive car service page explains the information to share for office, hotel and event transfers. For celebrations, use the wedding and event planning checklist.

Confirm the airport or private aviation terminal

For MIA, FLL or PBI, include the airline and flight number as well as the date. A scheduled landing time is not the same as the time you will be ready after baggage collection. Tell our team if you are arriving internationally or traveling with extra luggage.

For private aviation, specify the FBO name and address rather than only naming the airport. Our airport and private jet transfer service includes flight tracking and FBO coordination. Confirm your meeting point and ask how delays and waiting time will be handled for your booking.

Compare vehicles by the needs of your group

The Splendid Limo fleet includes the Cadillac Escalade, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Bentley Bentayga and Mercedes-Benz Maybach. Share the passenger count and luggage before settling on a vehicle. Do not assume that the number of seats tells you how much room will remain for bags.

If you need a particular amenity or accessibility arrangement, ask our team to confirm it for the vehicle available on your date. For a larger social outing, review the Miami party-bus packages and confirm capacity and inclusions before booking.

Read the whole quote

Ask what is included in the price and what may be charged separately. Useful questions cover waiting time, extra stops, parking, tolls, gratuity, payment terms and cancellation or itinerary changes. These are items to confirm, not a promise that a particular fee applies or is included.

Send the details once

Use the quote form to provide your date, passenger count and trip notes, or call 954-669-8444. Our team follows up by phone or text. Keep the final itinerary and agreed terms available for the day of travel; submitting the form alone does not confirm a reservation.

Plan your ride

Tell us your date, route and group size. We’ll follow up by phone or text to discuss your trip and quote.
