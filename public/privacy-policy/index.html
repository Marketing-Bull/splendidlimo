Information you give us

When you request a quote we collect the details you enter: your name, mobile phone number, the service you need, and optionally your pickup date, number of passengers and trip notes. We use them to contact you by phone or text message about your request and to provide and improve our service. Message and data rates may apply; you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Information collected automatically

When you send a quote request we also receive technical and marketing information about your visit: the pages you viewed, how you found us (referring site and campaign tags such as UTM parameters), your IP address and approximate location, device, browser, language, time zone and screen size, and how many times you have visited. To remember how you found us, our site stores a small record of this in your browser’s local storage. It is not used for advertising and is sent to us only if you submit a quote request.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand website visits and how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as page views, browser and device details, referring websites and interactions. We measure clicks on our call and text links and successful quote requests. Our custom analytics events do not include the name, phone number or trip notes entered in the quote form.

This analytics collection is separate from the first-party quote attribution described above and can occur without submitting a quote request. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites and the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal information. Quote requests are emailed to our team through our email delivery provider (Resend), and the site is hosted by Vercel and served through Cloudflare. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details.

How long we retain your data

We keep quote requests only as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, tax or administrative obligations.

What rights you have over your data

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop receiving calls or texts, by emailing us at the address above. You can clear the stored visit record at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.
