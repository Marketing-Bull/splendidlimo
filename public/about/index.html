The person behind the experience

Meet Eduard, the Man Behind Splendid Limo

Eduard—known as Eddie—built Splendid Limo around a simple idea: every trip should feel like a five-star experience.

Plan Your RideFollow @splendidlimo

Personal attention, from pickup to drop-off

For Eduard, the details are part of the experience: being punctual, helping with luggage, having cold water ready, and keeping the vehicle spotless. It is the same thoughtful approach whether a client is arriving for a business trip or celebrating a special occasion.

Clients describe him as professional, courteous, and willing to go the extra mile. That personal care is at the heart of Splendid Limo: treating every client like a VIP and every ride as a reflection of the company’s reputation.

South Florida, with a chauffeur who knows the occasion

Splendid Limo serves Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, and Palm Beach, with service available 24/7. Eduard and his chauffeurs handle airport and private jet transfers, executive travel, and weddings and special events.

For airport journeys, the team coordinates private aviation pickups and uses real-time flight tracking. For a night out, that local knowledge extends to South Beach and Brickell, concerts, and party-bus experiences.

A luxury fleet, with personal service behind it

The Splendid Limo fleet includes the Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Bentley Bentayga, Mercedes-Benz Maybach, Cadillac Escalade, and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter. Tell us about your occasion, group, and itinerary so the team can discuss an appropriate vehicle and confirm availability.

Choosing a vehicle is one part of the journey. The care behind it—the preparation, communication, and attention to your experience—is what Eduard wants clients to remember.
