Planning a Miami Party Bus Ride: Groups, Routes & Packages

By Splendid Limo & Transportation · Published 2024-07-31 · Updated October 22, 2026

A good group outing starts with a shared plan. Decide who is coming, where everyone will meet and how the night will end before comparing vehicles or packages. Splendid Limo offers Miami party-bus experiences with professional chauffeurs and customizable routes.

Compare the published packages

Our party-bus page lists the Ultimate Miami Night Out and Miami Beach Club Crawl packages, along with their per-person prices, minimum group sizes and stated inclusions. Read those details together rather than comparing only the headline price. Contact our team to confirm availability and the arrangements for your date.

A dinner stop, club visit or return journey can change the schedule. Confirm which venues, admission arrangements, refreshments and stops are included in your specific booking. If you want something different, ask before assuming it is covered.

Count passengers and choose a meeting point

Give us your expected group size and confirm the capacity of the vehicle available for your event. Decide whether everyone will meet at one address or whether you need several pickups. Include the complete addresses and ask how additional stops affect the quote.

Our party-bus videos let you look inside the available styles. They are a starting point for the conversation; confirm the actual vehicle for your booking with the team.

Plan the return before leaving

Share your intended finish time and drop-off location when requesting the ride. Make one person the main contact for the group and keep their phone available. If plans change during the evening, contact the team rather than assuming the original booking includes an open-ended itinerary.

Questions to ask before confirming

  • Which vehicle fits the group, and is it available on our date?
  • What is included in the quoted package, and what costs extra?
  • What venue admission, age or identification requirements apply?
  • How are additional stops, waiting time or a later return handled?
  • What payment and cancellation terms apply?

Planning a different kind of celebration?

For a ceremony, reception or birthday with several scheduled journeys, see our wedding and event transportation page. Guests arriving by plane can also request airport transfers. Share those journeys separately so each part of the day can be confirmed.

When your group and itinerary are ready, request a quote or call 954-669-8444. The team will follow up to discuss the details; the form does not reserve a vehicle by itself.
