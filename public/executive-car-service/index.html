From airport arrivals to your next meeting

Plan travel between airports, hotels, offices and event venues in Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and West Palm Beach. Share the complete itinerary, including the time you need to arrive at each stop, so our team can discuss your transportation needs.

For arrivals by commercial or private aircraft, our airport transfer service includes flight tracking and FBO coordination. Add your flight information when you request a quote.

Match the vehicle to your trip

Our fleet includes the Mercedes-Benz Maybach, Cadillac Escalade, Bentley Bentayga, Rolls-Royce Cullinan and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter. Vehicle availability depends on your date and itinerary. Tell us how many colleagues are traveling and how much luggage or event equipment you will bring so we can discuss an appropriate option.

Plan multiple stops before you book

A direct airport transfer and a day with several meetings need different arrangements. Include every planned stop and your expected finish time in the request. Ask our team to confirm the booking format, any waiting time, changes to the itinerary and the charges that apply before you commit.

  • Pickup date, time and address.
  • Meeting or venue addresses and required arrival times.
  • Passenger count, luggage and the main travel contact.
  • Return arrangements and any stops that may change.

Transportation for your clients and guests

We work with hotels, restaurants, private aviation services and businesses to coordinate guest transportation. If you arrange rides regularly or have a larger event, explore our corporate partnerships and contact the team about coordination and billing arrangements.

Confirm the details that matter to your team

When reviewing your quote, confirm the vehicle, itinerary, inclusions, payment terms and cancellation or change policy. If a particular amenity or accessibility arrangement is essential, ask us to confirm it for the assigned vehicle before booking.
